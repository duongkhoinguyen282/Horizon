Researchers have developed a new CRISPR-based technique using the Cas12a2 enzyme to selectively identify and destroy cancer cells. This method triggers a mechanism that shreds the cell's chromatin once a specific target sequence is detected. This breakthrough offers a potential pathway to treat cancers previously considered "undruggable" by traditional small-molecule drugs. It provides a more precise and destructive approach to eliminating malignant cells compared to older CRISPR methods. Unlike Cas9, which primarily creates targeted DNA breaks, Cas12a2 acts as a collateral nuclease that shreds DNA throughout the cell upon activation. This high level of destruction ensures the targeted cancer cell is effectively neutralized.

## [Adaptive Tokenisation Via Temporal Redundancy Masking And Latent Inpainting](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1u2u9bb/adaptive_tokenisation_via_temporal_redundancy/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The authors introduce a parameter-free mechanism that dynamically allocates video tokens by masking redundant latent positions based on temporal differences. A lightweight Latent Inpainting Transformer (LIT) is then used to reconstruct these dropped tokens without requiring auxiliary routing networks. This approach significantly improves computational efficiency in video generation by eliminating the need for iterative searches or complex regressors. It achieves substantial speedups over existing baselines while maintaining high reconstruction quality. The framework delivers a 31x inference-time speedup over ElasticTok-CV and a 2x speedup over InfoTok. It relies on a fixed threshold for temporal-L1 differences to identify redundant latent information.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/chhaya_35 · Jun 11, 09:32

**Background**: Adaptive tokenisation is a technique used to optimize computational resources by processing only the most informative parts of a visual sequence. In video models, temporal redundancy refers to the fact that consecutive frames often contain very similar information, which can be exploited to reduce the number of tokens processed without losing significant detail.
